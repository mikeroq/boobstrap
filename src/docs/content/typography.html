Hierarchy

Choose heading levels from the document outline. Use size classes to change appearance without skipping semantic levels.

Readability

Reserve .bs-display for short hero copy. Body and lead text keep more generous line height for sustained reading.

Emphasis

Weight, color, and italic utilities are presentational. Do not rely on color alone to convey a warning, error, or required state.
